Research

Questions I like spending serious time on

My interests are centered on machine learning problems where structure matters: sequential data, graphs, uncertainty, optimization, retrieval, and human-in-the-loop decision making.

Information Retrieval Graph Learning Active Learning Knowledge-Graph Reasoning Probabilistic Inference Adversarial Robustness

Current directions

LLM-augmented knowledge-graph reasoning

Literature review and exploration under Prof. Soumen Chakrabarti, with an emphasis on how language models can interact with explicit structured knowledge rather than replacing it.

Symmetric nonnegative embeddings

Studying representation quality and ablation behavior in embedding methods with Prof. Abir De, focusing on interpretable structure and empirical behavior.

Human-assisted active learning

Algorithmic work involving submodular maximization and Exp3-style querying to understand efficient learning under limited feedback and annotation budgets.

What draws me in

  • Models that must generalize under drift or uncertainty
  • Inference pipelines that expose useful structure
  • Optimization formulations that turn vague goals into tractable systems
  • Interfaces between theory and deployable ML systems

Breadth

Related areas I enjoy building in

Retrieval and ranking

Dense and sparse retrieval, reranking, diversity-aware ranking, and hybrid systems that perform well beyond a single benchmark setting.

Robust ML

Adversarial settings, transferability, out-of-distribution behavior, and model selection under changing data regimes.

Structured reasoning

Probabilistic graphical models, optimization-based formulations, and systems that benefit from explicit constraints rather than purely implicit learned behavior.
